Asked: What you asked the AI tool to do. The actual prompt, or a faithful summary of it if the exchange was long or iterative.
Produced: What it actually gave you. A short summary is fine; you don't need to paste the entire output if the commit diff already shows it.
Changed or rejected: What you kept as-is, what you edited, and what you threw out entirely, with a reason for each. This is the field that actually gets graded. "Looked fine" is not a reason.
